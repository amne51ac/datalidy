Strategy & Operations

Strategy and operations that work because the people who run them helped build them.

Discuss your needs
Overview

Why the people lens matters here.

The Strategy & Operations practice helps leadership teams set direction and build the organization that can deliver it. We work on strategic plans, operating models, process improvement, customer and innovation strategy, responsible AI adoption and talent strategy for companies, investor-backed businesses, nonprofits and public institutions. The common thread is execution: most strategies fail not because the analysis was wrong but because decision rights, workflows, incentives and capabilities were never redesigned to support them.

Our people lens is practical rather than sentimental. Every operating model is a set of decisions about who does what work, with what information, under whose authority. Every process improvement changes someone's day. Every AI deployment shifts tasks, skills and judgment between people and systems. We start by listening to the employees and customers who live inside those systems, measure where value and friction actually sit, and design changes with the people who will have to run them.

The result is strategy that is explicit about trade-offs, operations that are simpler to work within, and organizations that can adapt as conditions change. We bring the analytical discipline of a strategy firm and the implementation patience of an operating partner, and we hand over the dashboards, governance routines and internal capability needed to sustain progress after our engagement ends.

Focus areas

What we focus on.

01

Strategy that reaches the front line

A plan that lives in a board deck changes little. We translate strategic choices into priorities, objectives and key results, resource allocation and management routines that middle managers can use, and we test the plan with the employees and customers who will experience it before it is finalized.

02

Operations designed around people

We map how work actually flows, from customer request to delivery, using value stream mapping and service blueprinting to find handoffs, rework and waiting. Improvements are designed with frontline teams, which tends to produce changes that stick and surfaces the practical knowledge that never appears in a process manual.

03

Responsible adoption of new technology

AI and automation are operating model decisions, not only technology decisions. We help leaders choose use cases, set governance, assess workforce impact and plan reskilling and job redesign, so that productivity gains do not come at the cost of trust, fairness or the institutional knowledge that employees carry.
