Capital & Investment Advisory

Evidence-based advice on the people who determine whether an investment thesis holds.

Discuss your needs
Overview

Why the people lens matters here.

Capital & Investment Advisory serves private equity sponsors, impact investors, family offices and corporate acquirers at the moments when people decisions carry the most financial weight: before signing, in the first hundred days, during integration and ahead of exit. We assess leadership teams, size organizational risk, build value creation plans that account for the workforce, and design measurement frameworks that investors and limited partners can trust. Our work sits alongside financial, commercial and legal diligence, and it answers the question those workstreams rarely address directly: can these people deliver this plan?

The people lens matters because most investment theses depend on human behavior. Revenue growth assumes a sales team that stays and performs. Margin expansion assumes managers who can redesign work without losing the operators who hold institutional knowledge. Integration assumes two cultures can agree on how decisions get made. When these assumptions fail, they tend to fail quietly and late. We bring them into view early, using structured interviews, people analytics and organizational diagnostics, so that sponsors can price risk, plan for it and act on it.

We also hold a conviction that responsible ownership and strong returns are compatible. Companies that treat employees fairly, serve customers well and earn the trust of their communities tend to be more resilient businesses. For impact and ESG-minded investors, we help translate that conviction into credible measurement, without overstating results or adding reporting burden that teams cannot sustain. We aim for evidence that boards, managers and stakeholders can all use.

Focus areas

What we focus on.

01

Leadership is the thesis

Every deal model contains an implicit bet on a management team. We make that bet explicit by assessing leaders against the specific demands of the value creation plan, not against a generic competency list. Sponsors get a clear view of where the team is strong, where it is thin, and which gaps must be closed before the plan is credible.

02

Workforce risk is financial risk

Key person dependency, compensation misalignment, attrition in critical roles and unresolved cultural friction all show up eventually in EBITDA. We quantify these exposures where the data allows and describe them precisely where it does not, so that investment committees can weigh people risk alongside commercial and operational risk with the same rigor.

03

Impact that holds up to scrutiny

Impact and ESG claims face growing scrutiny from limited partners, regulators and the public. We help investors define outcomes that matter to employees, customers and communities, choose indicators they can collect consistently, and report with appropriate humility. The goal is measurement that informs decisions inside the portfolio, not only reporting that satisfies outside audiences.
