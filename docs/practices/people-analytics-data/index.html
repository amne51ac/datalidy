People Analytics & Data

Rigorous, ethical people analytics that help leaders make better decisions about their workforce.

Discuss your needs
Overview

Why the people lens matters here.

Our People Analytics & Data practice helps leaders answer the questions that matter most about their workforce: who is likely to leave and why, whether pay is fair, where collaboration is breaking down, what skills the strategy will require, and whether investments in people are paying off. We build the analyses, models, data foundations and dashboards that turn scattered HR, finance and operational data into evidence that executives, boards and investors can act on with confidence.

The people lens matters because workforce data describes human beings. Analysis done carelessly can expose individuals, encode historical bias into predictions, or reduce complex experiences to a single score. We treat privacy, consent, transparency and fairness as design requirements, not afterthoughts. We aggregate responsibly, test models for disparate impact, and explain findings in language that employees and managers can understand and trust.

Our work combines statistical depth with practical judgment. We use methods suited to the question, from regression-based pay equity models and survival analysis to organizational network analysis and text analytics, and we pair quantitative findings with listening so that numbers carry their context. Using our Listen, Measure, Design, Deliver, Sustain method, we leave clients with stronger internal capability, sound governance and measures they can maintain on their own.

Focus areas

What we focus on.

01

Decisions, not dashboards

Analytics earns its keep when it changes a decision. We start every engagement by defining the decisions leaders face and the evidence they would need to make them well, then work backward to the data and methods. That focus prevents the common pattern of building reports that are admired once and rarely opened again.

02

Ethics by design

People data carries obligations. We apply data minimization, minimum group sizes for reporting, role-based access and documented model governance, and we test predictive tools for fairness across demographic groups. Clear communication with employees about what is collected and why is part of the work, because trust is the precondition for honest data.

03

Numbers with context

A turnover rate or an engagement score rarely explains itself. We pair quantitative analysis with interviews, focus groups and open-text analysis so that leaders understand the mechanisms behind the metrics. This combination produces recommendations that address root causes and helps avoid confident action based on correlations that do not hold up.
