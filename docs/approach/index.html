Our approach

Listen. Measure. Design. Deliver. Sustain.

One disciplined method sits under every Datalidity engagement, from a two-week diligence sprint to a multi-year transformation. It joins the rigor of analytics with the depth of human-centered design.

Talk to us
01 · Listen

We start with the people closest to the problem.

Before we form a view, we listen: to executives and frontline staff, to customers and community members, to the people who are usually spoken for rather than heard. Our researchers use interviews, contextual inquiry, ethnographic observation, listening sessions and diary studies to understand what people actually experience and why.

  • Stakeholder and community interviews
  • Contextual inquiry and field observation
  • Listening sessions and focus groups
  • Document, policy and data review
02 · Measure

We turn experience into evidence.

Qualitative insight tells us what matters. Measurement tells us how much, for whom and at what cost. We build baselines from HR, operational, financial and customer data, field targeted surveys, and apply people analytics methods to separate signal from noise and size the opportunity.

  • People analytics diagnostics
  • Baseline metrics and KPI design
  • Survey design and statistical analysis
  • Equity and segmentation analysis
03 · Design

We design with people, not for them.

Using service design, experience design and organizational design, we develop solutions in partnership with the people who will live with them. Co-design workshops, prototypes and pilots let us test ideas cheaply and improve them before they are scaled.

  • Service blueprints and journey maps
  • Organizational and operating model design
  • Co-design workshops and prototypes
  • Pilot design and evaluation plans
04 · Deliver

We stay through the hard part.

Recommendations are only as good as their implementation. We support leaders through rollout with change management, communications, leadership coaching and capability building, adjusting the plan as evidence comes in.

  • Implementation roadmaps and governance
  • Change management and communications
  • Leader coaching and team enablement
  • Training and capability building
05 · Sustain

We leave you stronger than we found you.

Lasting change needs measurement and ownership. We build dashboards, governance routines and impact measurement frameworks, then transfer them to your team so progress continues and compounds after our engagement ends.

  • People and impact dashboards
  • Governance and review cadences
  • Impact measurement frameworks
  • Knowledge transfer and handover
Principles

How we hold ourselves accountable.

Rigor and empathy, together

Numbers without context mislead; stories without evidence don't scale. We insist on both.

The affected are experts

People who live with a problem understand it in ways no outsider can. We design with them.

Measure what matters

We define outcomes for employees, customers and communities, not just outputs and activity.

Responsible with data

People data is personal. We apply privacy, consent and ethical review to every analysis.

Build capability, not dependency

We transfer methods and tools so your team can continue the work without us.

Candor over comfort

We tell clients what the evidence shows, especially when it is uncomfortable.

Work with us

Let’s talk about the people problem in front of you.

Every engagement begins with a conversation. Tell us what you are facing and we will tell you, candidly, how we would approach it.
