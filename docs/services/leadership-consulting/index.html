The challenge

The problem behind the problem.

Most organizations invest in leadership development, yet many executives remain unsure whether it is working. Programs are often generic, disconnected from strategy and evaluated by participant satisfaction rather than changes in behavior or results. Leadership competency models sit in HR systems but rarely shape who gets promoted. Meanwhile, the demands on leaders keep shifting: hybrid teams, rapid technological change, tighter budgets and higher expectations from employees about fairness, flexibility and purpose.

The result is a familiar pattern. Senior leaders carry too much, middle managers are promoted for technical skill and given little support, and high-potential employees leave because they cannot see a path forward. Leadership becomes a bottleneck to strategy. Organizations need a clear, specific view of what leadership must look like to deliver their strategy, an honest assessment of where they stand, and development that changes how leaders actually behave in their daily work.

Our approach

How we deliver leadership consulting.

  1. 01Listen

    Connect leadership to strategy

    We interview executives, board members and a cross-section of managers and employees to understand the strategy, the leadership challenges it creates and how leaders are currently experienced. We pay particular attention to the gap between espoused leadership values and the behaviors that actually get rewarded.

  2. 02Measure

    Leadership capability baseline

    We assess current leadership capability using multi-rater feedback, engagement and retention data by manager, promotion patterns and, where useful, network analysis. This shows where leadership strength is concentrated, where it is thin and how leadership quality affects outcomes such as retention and performance. Findings are shared with leaders openly.

  3. 03Design

    A leadership framework people use

    We co-design a concise leadership framework that describes specific, observable behaviors for each level, from first-line manager to executive. We align it with selection, performance management and promotion, and design development journeys that combine learning, coaching, stretch assignments and peer cohorts tied to real business challenges.

  4. 04Deliver

    Development built around real work

    We deliver leadership programs, facilitate executive offsites and coach senior leaders. Participants work on live strategic problems, which builds capability and advances business priorities simultaneously. We train internal facilitators and HR partners so the organization can sustain the approach. Senior leaders teach in programs, which signals commitment and reinforces the behaviors the framework describes.

  5. 05Sustain

    Measure behavior change

    We track changes in multi-rater feedback, team engagement, retention and internal mobility to assess whether leadership is improving. Leaders and HR review this evidence together and adjust investment toward what works. Evaluation is planned from the outset with clear comparison points, so results are credible to executives and boards, and we report candidly when programs are not producing the change intended.

Who we serve

Who engages us.

Our leadership consulting clients include chief executives, chief people officers, heads of talent and leadership development, and boards. Impact lens

Beyond the balance sheet.

Leadership quality is one of the strongest influences on how people experience work. Better leaders create teams where people feel respected, understand their purpose and can grow. Those conditions reduce burnout and turnover and allow employees to serve customers with more care. In mission-driven organizations, stronger leadership translates directly into better services for patients, students or communities. Leadership consulting that focuses on fairness and inclusion also widens access to leadership roles for people who have historically been overlooked.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does a leadership consultant do?

A leadership consultant helps organizations define the leadership they need, assess current capability and build stronger leaders. Work can include developing leadership frameworks, designing development programs, coaching executives, facilitating leadership team sessions and aligning promotion and performance processes with leadership expectations. Good leadership consulting starts from strategy and measures whether leaders actually change how they work.

How do you measure the impact of leadership development?

We look beyond satisfaction surveys to changes in observable behavior, measured through multi-rater feedback before and after development, and to outcomes in leaders' teams, such as engagement, retention, internal mobility and performance. We set up the evaluation design at the start so that comparisons are meaningful, and we are candid when results are mixed.

Do you deliver off-the-shelf leadership programs?

No. We use proven content and methods, but every program is designed around the client's strategy, leadership framework and real business challenges. Participants work on live problems, apply learning between sessions and receive coaching. This takes more design effort than an off-the-shelf program but produces development that is relevant and more likely to transfer to daily work.

How do you develop middle managers?

Middle managers need clear expectations, manageable spans of control, practical skills in coaching and prioritization, and the authority to make decisions. We address both capability and conditions: development in core management skills alongside changes to role design, workload and decision rights. Peer cohorts help managers solve problems together and build networks across the organization.
