Portfolio Leadership Assessment

Portfolio leadership assessment for sponsors and boards who need an objective view of whether the CEO and management team can deliver the plan, and a clear set of development, hiring or succession actions.

The challenge

The problem behind the problem.

Sponsors routinely cite leadership as one of the most important determinants of portfolio outcomes, yet many still assess management teams informally, through deal team impressions, board meeting performance and reference calls. These signals are useful but incomplete. Leaders who present well may struggle to build teams or make hard operational choices, while quieter leaders may be underestimated. The cost of a delayed leadership change is high, and so is the cost of an unnecessary one.

The question is rarely whether someone is a good executive in general. It is whether this leader, and this team, can deliver this specific plan in this time frame. A founder who built the business to its current scale may or may not be the right person to professionalize it for its next phase. A CFO who managed a stable business may need significant support to run an add-on program and prepare for exit. Sponsors need assessments that answer those specific questions fairly and rigorously.

Our approach

How we deliver portfolio leadership assessment.

  1. 01Listen

    Define what the plan requires

    We start with the sponsor and board to translate the value creation plan into leadership requirements: the decisions, capabilities and behaviors each role must deliver over the hold period. We then conduct in-depth structured interviews with each executive, exploring their track record, judgment and approach to the challenges ahead.

  2. 02Measure

    Multi-source evidence

    We gather confidential input from direct reports, peers and board members, and use validated psychometric tools where appropriate. We review team dynamics by observing leadership meetings and analyzing how decisions flow. Evidence is assessed against the plan-specific requirements, not a generic leadership model. Findings are calibrated across assessors.

  3. 03Design

    Individual and team findings

    We present a clear view of each leader's strengths, risks and development needs, and an assessment of the team as a whole: capability gaps, overlaps, decision-making patterns and bench depth. Recommendations distinguish between development, role change, additional hires and succession planning. Each recommendation states its urgency and its link to plan risk.

  4. 04Deliver

    Feedback and development planning

    Each executive receives a constructive feedback session and a development plan, which helps make the assessment a tool for growth rather than only evaluation. Where changes are required, we help sponsors plan transitions thoughtfully, including role definitions for new hires and support for leaders moving on.

  5. 05Sustain

    Reassess as the plan evolves

    We help boards track development progress and revisit the assessment at key milestones, such as after a major add-on or ahead of exit preparation, so leadership capacity keeps pace with the business. Where several portfolio companies are assessed with the same method, sponsors also gain comparable data that reveals recurring leadership patterns across the portfolio and informs future investment decisions.

Who we serve

Who engages us.

Our leadership assessment clients include private equity operating partners, talent partners, deal leads and portfolio company boards, as well as family office and impact investors with board representation. Assessments are commonly commissioned shortly after acquisition, when a portfolio company misses plan, ahead of a significant add-on or transformation, before a CEO succession decision, or as part of exit preparation. Some sponsors use our approach as a standard step across every new platform investment.

Impact lens

Beyond the balance sheet.

Leadership decisions shape the daily experience of every employee in a portfolio company. Fair, evidence-based leadership assessment reduces the likelihood of abrupt, poorly explained changes at the top, and helps sponsors invest in developing leaders rather than simply replacing them. Strong management teams create better working conditions, more consistent service for customers and steadier employment for communities that depend on local businesses. When transitions are necessary, a thoughtful process treats departing leaders with respect and protects the organization from disruption.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do private equity firms assess management teams?

Practices vary widely. Some firms rely on deal team judgment and references, while others use structured interviews, multi-rater feedback and psychometric assessment. The most useful assessments evaluate leaders against the specific demands of the value creation plan and examine the team as a whole, not only individuals. Findings should lead to concrete actions: development, role changes, additional hires or succession plans.

Will a leadership assessment unsettle the management team?

It can if handled poorly. We are transparent with executives about the purpose and process, emphasize development as well as evaluation, and provide each leader with constructive feedback. Many executives value an objective view of their strengths and growth areas. The sponsor and board agree in advance how findings will be shared, which builds trust in the process.

Do you use psychometric tests?

Where appropriate, we use validated instruments to add insight on cognitive approach, personality and leadership style. They are one input among several and never the sole basis for a decision. Structured interviews, track record analysis and multi-source feedback carry most of the weight, because they speak more directly to how a leader will handle the plan's actual challenges.

Should the founder remain CEO after investment?

There is no general answer. Many founders lead successfully through institutional ownership, particularly with strong supporting executives and clear governance. Others are better suited to a chair, product or customer-facing role as the business professionalizes. Our assessment considers the founder's capabilities, motivation and fit with the plan, and helps sponsors and founders reach a decision that serves the business.
