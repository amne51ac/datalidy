The challenge

The problem behind the problem.

Private equity firms have built sophisticated capabilities for financial, commercial and operational diligence, yet the human factors that drive most value creation plans are often assessed informally. Management teams are judged through a handful of meetings, workforce data arrives late and incomplete in the data room, and culture is treated as a soft topic until it becomes a hard problem. Holding periods are compressed, debt is expensive, and there is little room to discover in month nine that the CFO cannot scale or that the sales force is quietly leaving.

Operating partners carry much of this burden across many portfolio companies at once, often without dedicated human capital resources. They need consistent methods for assessing leaders, sizing workforce risk, setting people priorities in the 100-day plan and tracking progress through the hold period. Limited partners are also asking more pointed questions about talent practices, employee outcomes and responsible ownership. The firms that answer those questions credibly, with data rather than anecdote, are better positioned both to create value and to raise their next fund.

Our approach

How we deliver private equity advisory.

  1. 01Listen

    Understand the thesis and the team

    We begin with the deal team and operating partners to understand the investment thesis, the value creation levers and the assumptions that depend on people. Where access allows, we conduct structured interviews with management, key employees and, when appropriate, customers, to understand how the business actually runs and where leadership capacity is concentrated or thin.

  2. 02Measure

    Quantify people risk and opportunity

    We analyze workforce data for attrition patterns, compensation competitiveness, key person dependency, spans and layers, and labor cost structure. We assess leaders against the demands of the specific plan. The result is a fact base that sits alongside financial and commercial diligence and translates people findings into implications for the deal model.

  3. 03Design

    Build the people agenda

    We translate findings into a prioritized people agenda: leadership changes or additions, retention arrangements for critical talent, management incentive plan design principles, organizational changes and culture priorities. Each item links to a value creation lever, carries an owner and is sequenced realistically within the 100-day plan and the broader hold period.

  4. 04Deliver

    Support execution through the hold

    We work alongside portfolio company leaders and operating partners to execute: running executive searches with defined success profiles, facilitating leadership team alignment, supporting organizational redesign and guiding managers through change. Our role is to build capability inside the company, not to create permanent dependence on advisors.

  5. 05Sustain

    Track and report what matters

    We establish a small set of people metrics for board and sponsor review, such as regretted attrition in critical roles, leadership bench strength and engagement in key populations. These indicators feed board packs, LP reporting and, eventually, the exit narrative, giving sponsors evidence that the people agenda is delivering.

Who we serve

Who engages us.

We work with deal partners, operating partners and heads of portfolio operations at middle-market and growth equity firms, as well as independent sponsors and family offices making control investments. Typical trigger moments include a new platform acquisition, a founder-led business moving to professional management, an underperforming portfolio company, a significant add-on that requires integration, and preparation for a sale process. We also support firms building their first in-house human capital function who want consistent methods across the portfolio.

Impact lens

Beyond the balance sheet.

Private equity ownership shapes the working lives of many employees, and the choices sponsors make about leadership, pay, structure and culture ripple outward to customers and communities. Our private equity advisory helps sponsors make those choices with better evidence. Retaining skilled employees, investing in managers and designing fair incentive structures tend to improve service quality and stability for customers. Responsible ownership practices also build trust with communities and limited partners, and they support more durable businesses that continue to perform after exit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does a private equity human capital advisor do?

A human capital advisor helps sponsors assess and improve the people side of an investment. That includes evaluating management teams, identifying workforce risks during diligence, building the people workstream of the 100-day plan, supporting leadership hires and organizational change, and tracking people metrics through the hold period. The aim is to make sure the leaders and workforce can deliver the value creation plan, and to surface risks early enough to act on them.

When should a PE firm bring in people advisors during a deal?

Ideally before signing, while there is still time to adjust price, structure or the plan. Pre-close work can be scoped to the access available, often a mix of data room analysis, management sessions and reference conversations. Many sponsors also engage advisors immediately after close to build the 100-day plan, or mid-hold when a portfolio company is underperforming and the root cause appears to be leadership or organizational rather than market-driven.

How is this different from executive search?

Executive search fills specific roles. Our work starts earlier and is broader: we assess whether the current team fits the plan, define what new roles truly require, and address organizational design, incentives and culture around those hires. When search is needed, we help define success profiles and assess finalists, and we can work alongside your preferred search firm. We have no placement fees, so our recommendations are not tied to hiring.

Can you work across an entire portfolio?

Yes. Many operating teams want consistent methods for management assessment, people diligence and people metrics across all portfolio companies. We can design a common toolkit, train deal and operating teams to use it, and provide on-demand support for individual companies. This creates comparable data across the portfolio and helps sponsors identify patterns, such as recurring leadership gaps, that inform future investment decisions.
