The challenge

The problem behind the problem.

Impact investors face a demanding dual mandate. They must generate the financial returns their investors expect while producing social or environmental outcomes that are real, attributable and meaningful to the people they are meant to benefit. Many funds articulate an impact thesis at launch but struggle to apply it consistently in deal selection, to support portfolio companies in deepening impact, or to separate genuine contribution from favorable trends. Without that discipline, impact claims become difficult to defend.

The people whose lives an investment is intended to improve are often absent from the process. Impact theses are written by investors, metrics are chosen for convenience and reporting flows upward to limited partners, rarely back to employees or communities. Meanwhile, expectations from asset owners and regulators continue to rise, and accusations of impact washing carry real reputational cost. Investors need practical ways to bring stakeholder voice into investment decisions and to manage impact with the same seriousness as financial performance.

Our approach

How we deliver impact investing advisory.

  1. 01Listen

    Ground the thesis in stakeholder voice

    We interview fund leadership, investment committee members and portfolio leaders, and, where appropriate, conduct listening sessions with workers, customers and community members affected by the strategy. This helps confirm whether the intended outcomes match what stakeholders actually need, and where the thesis may rest on untested assumptions.

  2. 02Measure

    Impact diligence and baselines

    We help build impact diligence into the investment process, assessing intended outcomes, depth and scale of effect, contribution and risk, using widely recognized dimensions of impact. For existing portfolios, we establish baselines on job quality, access and affordability, or other outcomes central to the thesis.

  3. 03Design

    Impact thesis and management system

    We co-design or refine the fund's theory of change, impact scoring approach, investment committee templates and portfolio engagement model. Where relevant, we help structure impact-linked terms or incentives. Each element is designed to be practical for deal teams and proportionate for portfolio companies with limited reporting capacity.

  4. 04Deliver

    Portfolio support and capability building

    We work directly with portfolio companies on impact-relevant improvements, such as job quality, inclusive hiring, customer access or community engagement, and train investment teams to apply the impact framework consistently. Support is tailored to company stage and sector. We emphasize practical changes that portfolio leaders can sustain with their own teams and budgets.

  5. 05Sustain

    Reporting and learning

    We help design LP reporting that is candid about progress and limitations, and internal review processes that feed learning back into future investment decisions. Where useful, we prepare funds for independent verification of their impact management practices. Learning reviews ask what worked, for whom and why, and feed those answers into the next round of investment decisions.

Who we serve

Impact lens

Beyond the balance sheet.

Impact investing exists to direct capital toward better outcomes for people and the planet, so the quality of impact management determines whether that purpose is fulfilled. By bringing workers, customers and communities into the design of theses and metrics, our impact investing advisory helps investments respond to real needs. Better job quality in portfolio companies benefits employees and their families. Improved access to essential products and services benefits underserved customers. Honest reporting strengthens trust across the field and helps more capital flow toward what works.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does an impact investing advisor do?

An impact investing advisor helps investors define what impact they intend to create, assess it during diligence, support portfolio companies in achieving it and measure and report results credibly. Work can include developing a theory of change, designing impact scoring for the investment committee, building stakeholder engagement into the process and preparing reports for limited partners. The goal is to manage impact with the same rigor as financial returns.

How do you avoid impact washing?

Credible impact rests on clear intended outcomes, evidence of contribution, honest reporting of shortfalls and input from affected stakeholders. We help investors define specific outcomes, distinguish their contribution from broader trends, choose indicators that reflect real change for people, and report transparently. Independent verification of impact management practices can provide additional assurance where investors seek it.

Which impact frameworks do you work with?

We work with the frameworks and standards our clients already use or that their limited partners expect, including widely adopted dimensions of impact, sector taxonomies and principles for impact management. We are framework-agnostic and focus on making whichever approach is chosen useful in practice, so that it informs investment and portfolio decisions rather than serving only as a reporting exercise.

Can you help a conventional fund develop an impact strategy?

Yes. Some conventional funds want to launch a dedicated impact vehicle, while others want to integrate impact considerations into an existing strategy. We help assess whether the firm's sourcing, expertise and portfolio support capabilities fit an impact mandate, define a credible thesis, and design the investment process changes required. We are candid when a proposed strategy is unlikely to produce the intended outcomes.
