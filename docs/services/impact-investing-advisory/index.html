Impact Investing Advisory

The challenge

The problem behind the problem.

Impact investors face a measurement problem that financial investors largely do not. Revenue and margin are well defined and audited. Social outcomes are not. Many impact reports rely on outputs, such as products sold, users reached or programs delivered, because those are easy to count. But reaching people is not the same as changing their circumstances. A digital health tool may be downloaded widely and used rarely. A financial inclusion product may expand access while leaving underlying behaviors unchanged. Without outcome evidence, impact claims are hard to verify and easy to overstate.

Frameworks help but do not solve the problem on their own. The Impact Management Project's five dimensions of impact and the IRIS+ catalog of metrics give investors a common structure for describing what, who, how much, contribution and risk. Applying them well requires research design: knowing which behaviors lead to the intended outcome, how to measure them reliably and how to separate a company's contribution from what would have happened anyway. Impact investing advisory grounded in behavioral science brings that rigor to diligence, monitoring and LP reporting.

Our approach

How we deliver impact investing advisory.

  1. 01Observe

    Understand the people served

    We begin with the people a company intends to benefit, using interviews, contextual inquiry and journey mapping to understand their circumstances, needs and the barriers they face. This grounds the theory of change in real experience and often reveals gaps between how a product is designed to help and how it is actually used.

  2. 02Signal

    Find the behaviors that create impact

    We analyze product, program and outcome data to identify the behaviors that lead to meaningful social results: sustained engagement, completion, adherence or changed financial habits. These become predictive impact KPIs, connected to the IMP five dimensions and, where appropriate, to IRIS+ metrics, so they can be compared and reported consistently.

  3. 03Design

    Build the measurement framework

    We design an impact measurement framework that fits the fund's thesis and each company's model: a clear theory of change, outcome and behavior indicators, data collection methods that do not overburden companies, and an honest approach to contribution and impact risk. Where impact can be improved, we also design product and service changes to strengthen it.

  4. 04Test

    Evaluate what works

    We design evaluations proportionate to the stage and resources of each company, from simple pre and post comparisons to pilots with comparison groups. Where companies are testing product changes, we help them include impact outcomes alongside commercial metrics, so they learn which features improve both returns and social results.

  5. 05Scale

    Report credibly to LPs

    We help funds turn portfolio data into impact reporting that LPs can trust: consistent metrics, clear distinction between outputs and outcomes, and candid discussion of limitations. We also build capability in portfolio companies so impact measurement becomes part of how they run the business, not a separate annual exercise.

Impact lens

Beyond the balance sheet.

This service exists because social good deserves the same rigor as financial return. Our impact investing advisory helps capital reach outcomes that matter to people: better health, more accessible education, greater financial security and stronger communities. We are careful about impact claims, because overstated impact damages trust in the whole field. And we apply behavioral science ethically, helping people do what they already want to do rather than steering them toward outcomes they did not choose.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What are the IMP five dimensions of impact?

The Impact Management Project framework describes impact through five dimensions: what outcome occurs, who experiences it, how much change happens, the contribution of the enterprise relative to what would have happened otherwise, and the risk that impact differs from expectations. It provides a shared language used widely by impact investors. We use it to structure measurement and reporting, supplemented by behavioral research on how outcomes come about.

How do you use IRIS+ in measurement frameworks?

IRIS+ is a widely used catalog of impact metrics and core metric sets organized by theme. We reference it to select standardized indicators where they fit, which supports comparability and LP reporting. We supplement standardized metrics with company-specific behavioral indicators when those better predict the outcomes that matter, and we explain how the two relate.

Can small portfolio companies realistically measure impact?

Yes, if the framework is proportionate. Early-stage companies cannot run large evaluations, but most can track a few behavioral indicators that lead to outcomes and collect simple outcome data from a sample of the people they serve. We design measurement that fits company resources and grows in rigor as the company scales, rather than imposing a burden that distracts from the business.

Do you help mainstream funds that are not impact funds?

Yes. Many private equity and venture firms want to understand the social effects of their portfolio companies, respond to LP questions or develop a responsible growth approach. We help them identify where portfolio companies affect employees, customers and communities, select practical indicators and integrate them into existing value creation and reporting processes without building a separate impact apparatus.
