About Datalidy

We exist to make the people side of business as rigorous as the rest of it.

Datalidy is a data-driven, social-impact-oriented advising and consulting firm. We help organizations solve the people problems that decide whether strategy succeeds, and in doing so, serve their customers, communities and the social good more fully.

Our mission

Better for people. Better for business. Better for communities.

Most organizations manage money, assets and operations with precision. Their people, the source of nearly every result, are too often managed by instinct. Leaders sense that culture is strained, that a team is not working, that customers are frustrated or that a community has lost trust, but lack the evidence to act decisively.

Datalidy was founded to close that gap. We believe decisions about people deserve the same standard of evidence as decisions about capital. We also believe evidence alone is not enough. Solutions must be designed with the people they affect, or they will not be adopted.

That is why our teams combine people analytics, organizational psychology, service design, experience design and strategy. We work across the full life of an organization, from the diligence before an investment to the leadership that carries it, the services that reach customers and the communities those services sustain.

Our values

What we stand for.

Validity

We are honest about what the data can and cannot tell us, and we never let a convenient story outrun the evidence.

Dignity

Every person in a system deserves to be understood, from the boardroom to the front line to the community beyond.

Shared value

We look for solutions where performance and social good reinforce each other, and we say so when they do not.

Who we serve

Our clients.

We work with leaders who believe that serving people well is both the right thing to do and the smart thing to do.

Investors

Private equity sponsors, impact investors and lenders who see people as the source of value creation and risk.

Executives and boards

CEOs, CHROs, COOs and directors leading growth, transformation, succession or integration.

Mission-driven institutions

Nonprofits, foundations, health systems, schools and public agencies serving communities.

Social enterprises

B Corps, cooperatives and purpose-led companies building business models around social good.

Work with us

Every engagement begins with a conversation. Tell us what you are facing and we will tell you, candidly, how we would approach it.
