About Datalidy

We make human behavior measurable, predictable and improvable.

Datalidy is a behavioral science and data advisory firm. We help investors, leaders and mission-driven institutions solve the people problems that decide performance, inside the business and beyond it, and we hold that work to the standard of the public good.

Our story

Applied behavioral science, built for decisions.

Datalidy works at the intersection of psychology, health, technology and business. Our practice brings together behavioral science, research methods, design and coaching, applied to the questions investors, executives and mission-driven leaders actually face.

Along the way we learned something simple. Organizations are drowning in data and starving for signal. The behaviors that actually predict conversion, adoption, adherence, engagement and leadership effectiveness are usually hiding in plain sight, buried in systems that were never integrated and covered by assumptions that were never tested.

So we built a way to find them. Our Behavioral Signals Design approach turns noisy data into predictive, behavior-based KPIs and clear experiments. Our coaching practice helps leaders and teams act on what the signals show. And our commitment to social impact keeps the work honest: behavioral science should help people do what they already want to do, never manipulate them.

Our values

What we stand for.

Validity

We are honest about what the data can and cannot tell us, and we never let a convenient story outrun the evidence.

Dignity

Every person in a system deserves to be understood and respected, from the boardroom to the front line to the patient, student or customer.

Shared value

We look for solutions where returns and the public good reinforce each other, and we say so plainly when they do not.
