What we believe

Performance is behavior.

Customer conversion, technology adoption, patient adherence, team engagement and leadership effectiveness are all behaviors, and behaviors leave signals. Predicting what people will do should not be an expensive guess.

Read our point of view

Our conviction
Most organizations are drowning in data and starving for signal. The few behaviors that truly predict outcomes are hiding in plain sight, waiting to be measured.

Science, not guesswork

Our work is grounded in behavioral science, clinical and health psychology, human-computer interaction and rigorous research methods.

Signals, not noise

We turn large, messy, unintegrated datasets into a handful of behavioral signals and predictive KPIs that leaders can act on.

Outcomes, not theory

We connect theory with measurable, real-world results through experiments, coaching and design that people actually adopt.

Signature methodology

Behavioral Signals Design.

Our proprietary approach to building more predictive KPIs. It finds the few human behaviors that drive an outcome and makes them measurable, testable and improvable.

01

Start from the outcome

Conversion, adoption, retention, adherence or performance: we anchor on the result that matters to the business and the people it serves.

02

Map the journey

We trace the full experience and the data around it, often across systems that were never designed to talk to each other.

03

Find the signals

We isolate the key conversion moments and behavioral types, and surface the untested assumptions quietly filling the gaps.

04

Predict and test

We define predictive, behavior-based KPIs and clear experiments to improve them, so progress is visible before lagging results arrive.

Our approach

Observe. Signal. Design. Test. Scale.

Every engagement follows one disciplined method that pairs qualitative depth with quantitative rigor, so recommendations are both right and adopted.

Observe

Interviews, contextual inquiry and user testing reveal what people actually do, not what they say.

Signal

Behavioral data analysis finds the few moments and behavioral types that predict the outcome.

Design

Behavioral, experience and service design shape the environment so the right behavior is easier.

Test

Experiments, A/B tests and pilots replace untested assumptions with evidence.

Scale

Coaching, dashboards and predictive KPIs make the gains durable and visible.
